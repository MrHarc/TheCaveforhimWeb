04 — The Experience

Craft Over Speed

01

The Consultation

We ask first. Your hair, your head shape, how you wear it on a Tuesday morning. Then we start.

02

The Cut

Clippers, scissors and a straight razor, used in the order the cut requires. Fades taken down clean, lines set square.

03

The Finish

Hot towel, product chosen for your hair rather than for the shelf, and a finish you can repeat at home.

04

The Room

Low light, red leather, and no rush. Booked time is your time.

A good cut should still look right on day nineteen.

Good grooming never goes out of style.
