# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local residents and visitors in La Cala de Mijas who want a precise haircut, beard service, traditional shave, or grooming appointment. Most arrive on a phone with a practical goal: understand the quality and atmosphere of the shop, confirm where it is, and book with minimal friction.

## Product Purpose

Present The Cave as a distinctive, trustworthy local barbershop and turn interest into a confirmed appointment. The site should make the real room, real work, services, prices, location, and booking options immediately legible without inventing claims or hiding important details.

## Brand Personality

Precise, assured, atmospheric.

## Anti-references

- Generic salon templates, lifestyle stock photography, and interchangeable luxury branding.
- AI-generated imagery or invented business claims.
- Loud gimmicks that compete with the shop, its work, or the booking action.
- Tattoo-shop or nightlife styling; neither is part of the verified offer.
- A corporate product or dashboard aesthetic that makes the local physical business feel abstract.

## Design Principles

- Let the real place and real work carry the story.
- Make craft feel deliberate, never slow or fussy.
- Keep booking and WhatsApp obvious without letting calls to action overwhelm the atmosphere.
- Prefer verified specifics over promotional generalities.
- Preserve a coherent dark, tactile identity across every route and device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep primary controls at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, preserve complete keyboard operation and visible focus, support 200% text without clipping or overlap, respect reduced-motion and data-saving preferences, retain a non-animated hero fallback, and account for iPhone safe areas and dynamic browser chrome.
